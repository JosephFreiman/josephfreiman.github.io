Mission 01

Star Wars Horde

C++ · RaylibSolo project

A solo survivor game built in C++ with Raylib, in the style of Vampire Survivors. Survive endless, escalating waves of Stormtroopers and Emperor's Royal Guards while auto-firing and switching between three iconic weapons — Luke's blaster, Chewbacca's bowcaster, and Anakin's lightsaber — each with its own firing style and damage. Built on multiple design patterns with a timed, multi-spawn enemy system.

Mission 02

Sorting Visualizer

C++ · RaylibSolo project

A solo project built in C++ with Raylib that shows how different sorting algorithms work — bubble, selection, and heap sort among them — animating every comparison and swap as it happens. You can switch between algorithms with a key press, and when a sort finishes it reports the total comparisons and swaps it made.

Mission 03

Networked Coop Interactions

Unreal Engine 5.6 · C++Group project

A two-player cooperative multiplayer prototype built in Unreal Engine 5.6 with C++, in collaboration with an external game studio (studio and game details under NDA). My part focused on researching the peer-to-peer connection, plus the co-operative carry mechanic, level design and assets, and a backdoor. The team also built the EOS networking (session hosting/joining and host migration), game-state persistence, co-op puzzle interactions, NPC perception AI, mic-driven lip sync, proximity voice chat with a walkie-talkie effect, and Gameplay Ability System movement effects.
